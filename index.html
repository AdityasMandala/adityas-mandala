        The Twelve Adityas

        What Is a Mandala

        Mandala — Sanskrit for "sacred circle" — is one of humanity's oldest and most universal spiritual symbols, representing wholeness: the self, the cosmos, and the cycles of nature unified in radial symmetry around a central point. Our twelve-leaf mandala places each Aditya at their precise position in the sacred wheel.

        The Gayatri Mantra

        Om Bhur Bhuvaḥ Svaḥ · Tat Savitur Vareṇyam · Bhargo Devasya Dhīmahi · Dhiyo Yo Naḥ Pracodayāt.

        "We meditate on the divine light of that adorable sun — may it illuminate our intellect." The Gayatri Mantra is the most revered prayer of the Vedic tradition, addressed to Savitur, chanted at dawn as a daily invocation of clarity.
